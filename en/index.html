Give me a poke

Hi, I’m Wuffel.

Tamdotti is a calm 3D pet game. You look after your Dotti, make a room for it and head out into the garden together.

Coming soon for iPhone and Android.

Home

A room you share.

Bed, rug, lamp, plant: you arrange everything just the way you like it. Feeding, bathing and cuddles all happen right here.

Garden

Something’s growing outside.

In the beds you grow strawberries, lettuce and pumpkins. You fetch water from the river, and whatever you harvest goes into your bag as a treat.

Online meadow

And sometimes there’s company.

If you like, you sign in and meet other Dottis on the meadow. You wave to each other, ride the merry-go-round or sit together on the bench. You can also visit friends and have little duels.

What you won’t find in Tamdotti

Ads
No banners, no videos in between.
Real-money buys
You earn coins by caring and playing.
Forced sign-up
You only need an account for friends and the online meadow.
